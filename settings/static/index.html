How savings are calculated

Quality-baseline savings compare uncached costs. Cache savings compare the chosen model’s uncached and measured cache-adjusted costs. Switch economics instead forecasts cost to completion: the incumbent stays warm, while a new candidate pays a cold first turn before later warm turns unless that candidate still has a live cache.

Daily totals, per-model aggregates, and up to 200 recent numeric switch decisions are stored in ~/.octopus/insights.json. Prompts, responses, session identifiers, and credentials are never recorded.
