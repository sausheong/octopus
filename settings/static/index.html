How savings are calculated

Routing savings compare uncached costs. Cache savings compare the chosen model’s uncached and measured cache-adjusted costs. Classifier usage is subtracted as overhead. Figures are estimates based on configured catalog prices and provider-reported tokens.

Only daily totals and per-model aggregates are stored in ~/.octopus/insights.json. Prompts, responses, session identifiers, and credentials are never recorded.
